# Homepage photo placeholders

The homepage uses remote Unsplash photo URLs in `app/home.css` for the hero and three feature cards. These are illustrative stock photographs, **not photos of DKPS students or facilities**.

Before public launch, confirm each image's current licensing and suitability, and preferably download approved copies into `public/images/` and update the CSS URLs to local paths. Remote photos require an internet connection and may become unavailable. Replace them with real school photographs when available. No AI-generated people were added.

The rest of the site, its API routes, and existing portal pages were not modified for this homepage redesign.
